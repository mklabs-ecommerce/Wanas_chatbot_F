# Exchange & Refund Policy

## Opening Packages at the Door

Packages may be opened at the door upon delivery so you can check them properly. If there is any issue, you can give the item back to the courier on the spot.

- Returns are only accepted through the courier at the time of delivery.
- If you return the item at the door, you only pay the shipping fees.

## Exchanges

If you would like to request an exchange, please contact us after receiving the package. We will ensure your exchange is processed as quickly as possible.

**Conditions:**
- Exchange requests must be initiated within **24 hours** of receiving your order.
- The item must remain in its **original packaging**, **unworn**, and **clean**.

**Who covers the shipping fees:**

| Reason for exchange | Who pays |
|---|---|
| Item is defective or you received the wrong item | Covered by the company |
| Personal preference (size, color, mind change, etc.) | Covered by the customer |

- For customer-preference exchanges, an **extra 20 EGP** is charged for exchange shipping, **in addition to** the regular shipping fees.

## Cancelling Orders

Order cancellation is allowed **before the order is shipped**. Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled — an exchange (per the terms above) would apply instead.
